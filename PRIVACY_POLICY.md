# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[LEGAL NAME]** ("EasySpeak", "we", "us", or "our") collects,
uses, and shares information when you use the EasySpeak mobile app and website (together, the
"Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not
use the Service.

## Summary

- EasySpeak is a speaking-practice app. Its core job is to record you speaking, transcribe it,
  and give you automated feedback — so we necessarily process your **voice recordings** and the
  **transcripts** of them.
- We collect **only** what we need to run the Service: your account details, your practice
  content, and your settings.
- We do **not** show ads, we do **not** use analytics, advertising, or cross-app/website tracking
  technologies, and we do **not** sell or share your personal information for advertising.
- Your recordings and transcripts are sent to trusted providers (Deepgram and OpenAI) solely to
  transcribe and generate feedback, and stored in our backend (Supabase).
- You can delete individual sessions or your entire account at any time.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your **email address** and a
**display name** (first name) you choose. Your password is handled by our authentication provider
and stored only in hashed form; we never see or store your plaintext password.

**Voice recordings and practice content.** When you record a practice session, we collect and
store:

- the **audio recording** of your voice;
- a **transcript** of that recording;
- the **prompt, topic, statement, or word** the session was about (whether generated by the app
  or entered by you);
- **delivery metrics** computed from your speech (for example speaking pace, filler-word count,
  pauses, and intonation) and an overall **score**;
- **AI-generated feedback** on the session.

These are saved to your practice history so you can review past sessions and track progress.

**Vocabulary content.** If you use the Vocabulary feature, we store the **words you add** and
their definitions. To fetch a definition, the word you look up is sent to a dictionary API
(Datamuse). Your recorded attempts at describing a word are handled the same way as other
practice content above.

**Settings and preferences.** We store the practice settings you choose — such as your target
speaking pace, custom filler words, practice focus, and your daily reminder time (if enabled).
Some of these, like your reminder time, are also stored locally on your device.

**Practice history and streaks.** We store timestamps and your local practice dates so we can
show your history and calculate your streak.

**Technical information needed to run the Service.** We process authentication tokens to keep you
signed in, and basic server-side logs needed to operate and secure the backend. We do not use
these to build a profile of you.

## Information we do NOT collect

To be clear about what the Service does **not** do:

- No third-party **analytics, advertising, or tracking** SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-website
  tracking.
- No **advertising identifiers** (e.g., IDFA) and no ad targeting.
- No **precise location**, **contacts**, **photos**, or **health** data.
- We do **not sell** your personal information, and we do **not share** it for cross-context
  behavioral advertising.
- Your voice recordings are used only to transcribe and analyze **your** speech for **your**
  feedback. We do not use them to identify you biometrically or build a voiceprint.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:

- provide the core features — transcribing your recordings, scoring your delivery, generating
  feedback, and saving your practice history, streaks, and vocabulary;
- create, secure, authenticate, and manage your account, and send account-related emails such as
  sign-up verification and password-reset codes;
- send you a daily practice reminder **only if you enable it** (this is a local notification
  scheduled on your device; see "Notifications" below);
- maintain, debug, secure, and improve the Service.

**Legal bases (EEA/UK users).** Where GDPR applies, we process your information to **perform our
contract** with you (providing the Service), based on your **consent** (for example, enabling
reminders), and for our **legitimate interests** in operating, securing, and improving the
Service.

## Notifications

If you turn on practice reminders, the app schedules a **local** daily notification on your
device at the time you choose. This is generated on your device by the operating system; no
push server is involved and no additional data is sent to us or a third party to deliver it. You
can turn reminders off in the app or in your device's Settings at any time.

## Storage, security, and retention

Your account data, practice content, and recordings are stored with Supabase. We use
industry-standard measures to protect your information, including **encryption in transit**,
**hashed passwords**, and **row-level security** that scopes your data so it is accessible only
to your own account. Recordings are kept in a private storage area and served only via
short-lived, signed links.

We retain your information while your account is active. When you **delete a session**, its
recording and associated data are removed. When you **delete your account**, your associated data
